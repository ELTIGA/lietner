# Code of Conduct

Lietner adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **hello@lietner.app**. Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainer, who may remove comments, close threads or block participants, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
