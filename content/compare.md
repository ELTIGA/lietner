## How do Lietner, Anki, Quizlet and Leitner box apps compare?

In short: Anki is the most powerful and the most to learn. Quizlet is the most social and has the most study games, with ads unless you pay. Leitner box apps keep things simple but vary widely. Lietner is a free, ad-free Leitner box with no setup, text-only cards and no sharing.

This page isn't a ranking. Each tool is the right choice for someone, and the table is here to help you work out which one is right for you.

## What should I check in a Leitner box app?

"Leitner box app" covers lots of small apps, so the last column can't be specific. Before you put months of cards into one, check:

- **Can you get your cards out?** Look for a CSV or text export. If the app closes, you want your deck back.
- **Does it sync, and where?** A phone-only app with no backup can lose everything with the phone.
- **What happens when you miss a card?** Classic Leitner sends it back to box 1. Some apps only move it down one box.
- **Is the price clear?** Look for subscriptions hidden behind a free download.
- **Does it handle your language?** Right-to-left scripts and accented letters can trip up some apps.

## Which one should I pick?

- **Pick Anki** if you need images, audio or cloze cards, or a big shared deck for medicine or law, or you'll keep thousands of cards for years. More in [Lietner as an Anki alternative](/anki-alternative).
- **Pick Quizlet** if your class or teacher uses it, or you like games and multiple choice. More in [Lietner as a Quizlet alternative](/quizlet-alternative).
- **Pick Lietner** if you want the Leitner system with no ads, no account and no setup, on any device. [Open Lietner](/) to try it in a minute.
