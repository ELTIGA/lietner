Lietner · spaced repetition

Free Leitner-system flashcards on a 3D desk

Lietner is a flashcard app that runs the Leitner system on a wooden desk in your browser. It is made for students 13 and up, it is free and open source, it works offline, and you don’t need an account.

How it works

  1. Your cards sit in five boxes. Box 1 comes due every day, then boxes 2 to 5 every 2, 4, 8 and 16 days.
  2. Knew it moves a card up one box, so you see it less often.
  3. Missed sends it back to box 1, due again tomorrow.

Lietner is the app; the Leitner system is the study method it’s built on.
